## Why

Minutes detected the transcript language on the Mac (`NLLanguageRecognizer`) and told the model which language to
write in. On 2026-10-06 this failed twice: a Spanish call was summarized in English despite the explicit instruction,
and another Spanish call was detected as Polish (the `**[HH:MM:SS] Speaker:**` prefixes confused the recognizer) and
summarized in Polish. The model sees the whole transcript and is a better judge; the user needs a manual override for
when it still gets it wrong.

## What Changes

- Minutes no longer detects the language. In Auto, the prompt asks the model to write in the language most of the
  transcript is spoken in, including the section headings, even though the instructions are in English.
- A "Language" menu next to Regenerate in the meeting detail, with Auto, English and Español.
- Choosing a language regenerates the summary in that language and re-tags the meeting, so topic tags follow it.
- The choice is stored with the meeting and used by every later regeneration, correction and re-tag. Auto removes it.
- New meetings use Auto; there is no global language setting.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
None.

### Modified Capabilities
- `meeting-summaries`: the model judges the language in Auto; the summary language can be fixed per meeting.
- `meetings-window`: the detail layout and the summary controls gain the Language menu.
- `meeting-tags`: topic tags follow the meeting's chosen language, else the model's judgment.
- `meeting-notes`: the sidecar stores the chosen language.

## Impact

- Code: `MeetingLanguage` (detection removed), the meeting detail summary bar, the sidecar model, the language used
  by summary, title and tag requests.
- No new setting, permission, dependency or network host. Note format unchanged (the choice lives in the sidecar).
- Titles are not regenerated by the menu; editing a title is a separate change (`add-editable-meeting-title`).
